Entertainment Buzz

Ramayana: Part 1, directed by Nitesh Tiwari and produced by Namit Malhotra, is gearing up for its monumental worldwide theatrical release on November 5, 2026. Promoted internationally as Ramayana: Rise of a Legend, the film features a star-studded ensemble cast including Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram, Sai Pallavi as Sita, Yash as the formidable Ravana, and Sunny Deol as Hanuman. Budgeted at a staggering ₹4,000 crore, making it the most expensive Indian film ever made, the cinematic spectacle features groundbreaking visual effects by DNEG and a landmark musical score co-composed by Oscar winners A.R. Rahman and Hans Zimmer. Anticipation is reaching a fever pitch as advance ticket sales have already crossed the ₹20 crore milestone just ten days ahead of its debut, signaling a potentially record-breaking opening at the global box office. While the first installment is set to dominate theatres this Diwali season, reports indicate that the second part—initially slated for late 2027—has been strategically pushed to a Diwali 2028 release to allow for the extensive production work required to complete the two-part saga.
